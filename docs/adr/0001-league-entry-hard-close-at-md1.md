# ADR-0001 — League-phase entry hard-closes at the first MD1 kickoff (D1)

Status: accepted · 2026-07-04 · PRD §4.1, §21-D1

## Context

Someone discovering the product after MD1 is locked out of the league phase for five
months. Alternatives: rolling entry with missed matchdays scoring 0, or pro-rata
pricing.

## Decision

Full Season entry closes at the first MD1 kickoff (8 Sep 2026), hard. No late league
entry in any form. Latecomers get a dedicated product: the Knockout pass (ADR-0004).

## Consequences

Everyone in the League Pool predicted the same 144 matches — the fairness story stays
one sentence. Maximum urgency in the pre-season window. The contract's stage model
stays trivial (no missed-matchday machinery in Stage 1). Post-MD1 demand is served by
continuous knockout sales.
