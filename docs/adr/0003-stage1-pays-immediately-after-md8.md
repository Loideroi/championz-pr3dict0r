# ADR-0003 — Stage 1 pays out immediately after MD8 finalizes (D3)

Status: accepted · 2026-07-04 · PRD §4.3, §21-D3

## Context

The league phase ends 27 Jan 2027; the final is 5 Jun 2027 — four months between
Stage 1 being decided and the season ending. Holding funds means a larger honeypot for
longer and a dead February.

## Decision

Freeze Stage 1 as soon as the last MD8 result clears its 24h provisional window
(~early Feb 2027) and open claims immediately.

## Consequences

Winners get real CHZ mid-season — the payout announcement *is* the marketing for the
knockout window's final weeks. User funds spend four fewer months in the contract. The
provisional window doubles as a built-in dispute buffer before the freeze; no separate
challenge period needed.
