# ADR-0008 — Ultimate ₵h@mpi0n wins a zero-fund trophy NFT (D8)

Status: accepted · 2026-07-04 · PRD §4.1, §21-D8

## Context

The Season View (combined Stage 1 + Stage 2 table) needs a payoff that makes the
two-stage season feel like one story. A purely cosmetic title feels thin for a crypto
product; a funded side-pot dilutes the headline pools and reopens fairness questions
(latecomers funding a prize they can't realistically win).

## Decision

Mint one minimal ERC-721 trophy NFT to the best combined season score, plus a profile
crown and a permanent hall-of-fame page. Zero funds attached.

## Consequences

Real, ownable, screenshot-able bragging rights with no impact on pool maths and a
negligible pentest surface. Latecomers remain formally eligible but structurally
unlikely to win it — which is exactly the early-bird flex the two-pronged design
promises.
